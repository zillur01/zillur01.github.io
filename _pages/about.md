Hello and welcome to my personal website! My name is Zillur Rahman, and I am currently working as a Research Engineer at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. Before that, I worked at leading self driving car companies like Waymo and Motional. I obtained my Master of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas.  I received my Bachelor of Science(BSc) degree from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET) in 2021. My research interests lie in the areas of machine learning, computer vision, and autonomous systems, and I am constantly exploring new technologies and techniques in these fields. You can learn more about my research here.
