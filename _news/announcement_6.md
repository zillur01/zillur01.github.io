Paper accepted in IEEE International Conference on Vehicular Electronics and Safety (ICVES 2022). In that study, we demonstrated how to implement a pedestrian emergency braking system in a very short time. A yolov5-based monocular 3D object detection model was used to measure the pedestrian distance and a longitudinal PD controller was designed to control the car speed smoothly. Finally, we compared both simulation and real-world performance.
