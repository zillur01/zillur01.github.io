The initial findings of my MS thesis have been accepted at the 2023 IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC). In this study, we present a new lane detection dataset created from challenging Las Vegas streets. Besides, we propose a lane classification model that can be used with a lane detection model to classify lane marking types such as solid and dashed. We also investigate the effect of using mixed-precision techniques in model training and testing.
